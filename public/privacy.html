Privacy Policy

Zune Feedback, the feedback form on zune-lab.github.io/zune-portfolio. Last updated: October 2026.

This site is a personal portfolio run by Zune. The feedback form lets you send me a message. To make sure a reply reaches the real owner of the email address, the form asks you to sign in with Google first. This page explains exactly what that involves.

What I receive

  • Your Google account email address, verified by Google when you sign in.
  • The message you write, and the optional "liked it / not a fan" mood you pick.
  • The time you sent it, and how long the form was open (used only to filter out bots).

Signing in uses only the basic openid and email information. I do not receive or store your name, profile photo, contacts, or any other Google data. I cannot see your password. I do not log your IP address, and I do not use cookies or analytics for this form.

How it is used

Only to read your feedback and, if I want to, reply to you at that email address. Nothing else: no marketing, no newsletters, no profiling.

Where it is stored

Messages are processed by a Google Apps Script owned by me and saved in a private Google Sheet and my email inbox, both under my own Google account. They are not published anywhere on this site. I do not sell or share this data with third parties, except that Google itself processes it as the platform hosting the script and sign-in.

How long I keep it

I keep feedback only as long as it is useful to me, and delete it whenever you ask.

Your choices

  • You can leave the form unused. Browsing the rest of the site never requires signing in.
  • To have your message and email deleted, contact me via github.com/Zune-lab and I will remove them.
  • You can revoke this site's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will appear on this page.
